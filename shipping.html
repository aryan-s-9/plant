Shipping & Logistics

Shipping Information

At Plantura Exports, we ensure that your agricultural products reach global destinations safely, on time, and in perfect condition.

🚢 Shipping Methods

  • Sea Freight – Ideal for bulk commodities like grains, pulses, oilseeds, and jaggery.
  • Air Freight – Perfect for fresh fruits, vegetables, and perishable items requiring fast delivery.
  • Courier & Express Cargo – For small orders and product samples.

📦 Packaging & Handling

  • International standard packaging to preserve freshness.
  • Palletization and fumigation for compliance with global norms.
  • Reefer containers (temperature-controlled) available for perishables.

📑 Shipping Documents

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Phytosanitary & Fumigation Certificates
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Insurance (if required)

🌐 Our Process

  1. Order confirmation & shipping terms finalization (FOB / CIF / CFR).
  2. Booking with reliable shipping lines & airlines.
  3. Customs clearance by our authorized agents.
  4. Safe loading and dispatch to the destination port/airport.
  5. Buyer receives all shipping documents for smooth clearance.

✅ Why Choose Us?

  • Hassle-free end-to-end logistics support.
  • Partnerships with leading freight forwarders & shipping lines.
  • Flexible options for both bulk and small shipments.
  • Timely delivery with full transparency.
