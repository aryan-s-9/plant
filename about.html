About Plantura Exports

Welcome to Plantura Exports – your trusted partner in agricultural trade.

Our journey began on 12th March 2025, when we started with local sales in India. With consistent quality and customer trust, we quickly grew in the domestic market. Now, we are excited to expand our horizons and step into the global export market, bringing India’s finest agricultural produce to buyers worldwide.
